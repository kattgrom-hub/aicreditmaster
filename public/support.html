Customer Support

Help with purchases and member access

Paid but access is not showing?

Sign in using the exact same email address used at Stripe Checkout. Access is matched to the purchaser email. If you have just paid, refresh the member dashboard after a short moment.

Account confirmation

If you created a new account, check your inbox and spam/junk folder for the Supabase confirmation email before signing in.

Payment or refund issue

Keep your Stripe receipt and Checkout email handy. It contains the transaction information needed to identify your purchase. For a duplicate charge, incorrect charge, failed access or other purchase problem, use the merchant contact details shown on your Stripe receipt and include the email used at checkout.

Security

Never send a password, full card number or security code in a support message. AI Credit Mastery will never need those details to locate your purchase.
