Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026

This policy explains how AI Credit Mastery handles personal information used to provide the website, checkout and member access.

We may receive your name, email address, account authentication information, purchase details, program tier and support information you choose to provide. Payment card details are processed by Stripe and are not stored by AI Credit Mastery.

We use information to process purchases, provide and secure member access, match purchases to entitlements, provide support, prevent abuse, maintain records and improve the service.

Service providers

We use third-party providers including Stripe for payments, Supabase for authentication/database services and Vercel for website hosting and infrastructure. Those providers process information according to their own privacy practices and applicable law.

Analytics

We may use privacy-focused website analytics to understand aggregate traffic and site performance. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and protect the service. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request help with account access, correction or deletion of information where applicable by using the support options on the site. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting reasons.

International processing

Some service providers may process data outside Australia. By using the service, you understand that information may be processed in jurisdictions where our providers operate.
