Credit Report Review Checklist

Request the right reports, separate unfamiliar information from a genuine error, and keep a clean evidence trail before asking for a correction.

1 · Request

Get the reports that may hold your information

Request your consumer credit reports directly from the reporting bodies that hold your information. Different bodies may hold different data.
Use the free access route. The OAIC says a credit reporting body must provide free access once every three months, with additional free access in some circumstances.
Use each provider's official website and complete its identity-check process. Do not send identity documents through social-media messages.

Official starting points: OAIC access guide and Moneysmart credit reports guide.

2 · Review

Check the facts line by line

Personal details: full name, date of birth and current/previous addresses.
Credit accounts: lender, account type, opening date, limit and status.
Repayment history and defaults: compare dates and amounts with your statements and correspondence.
Credit enquiries: identify the provider, date and application you made.
Public-record information and serious credit infringements: verify that the entry belongs to you and is current.
3 · Classify

Do not dispute an entry merely because it is unfamiliar

Check whether a trading name, broker, debt purchaser or related company explains the unfamiliar name.
Mark each item: confirmed correct, needs more information, or supported error.
For a suspected error, write the exact field, why it appears wrong, the correct information and the evidence you have.

Accurate negative information cannot legitimately be removed simply because it is inconvenient. This checklist is for understanding reports and correcting genuine inaccuracies.

4 · Record

Build a simple evidence log

Reporting body: ____________________   Report date: __________

Entry or account: ______________________________________________

What appears wrong: ____________________________________________

Evidence held: _________________________________________________

Correction sent to: ____________________   Date: __________

Reference number: ____________________   Outcome: __________

5 · Correct

Use the free correction process

Contact the credit reporting body or the relevant credit provider and request correction of the specific error.
Keep copies of the request, supporting documents, confirmation and reference number.
If you cannot resolve the issue or are unhappy with the outcome, review the free complaint pathways explained by Moneysmart and the OAIC.
