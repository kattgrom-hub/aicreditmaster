# AI Credit Mastery - three human-led short-video scripts

Use Katy on camera for the opening and closing. Insert a screen recording of the official page or the free checklist in the middle. Do not show a real credit report, address, date of birth or account number.

## 1. How to request your Australian credit reports

**Hook (0-4s):** "Before you pay anyone to fix your credit, get the facts for free."

**Body (5-32s):** "In Australia, you can request your consumer credit report free from the reporting bodies that hold your information. The OAIC says the free-access route is available once every three months, with extra access in some situations. Start with the OAIC or Moneysmart pages, follow the official provider links, and complete the identity check directly. Different bodies can hold different information, so one report may not tell the whole story."

**Close (33-45s):** "I made a free Australian Credit Report Review Checklist. Use it to request, compare and record what you find. Link in the description."

**On-screen text:** Free report route | Official sites only | Check more than one body

**CTA:** View the free checklist.

## 2. Unfamiliar entry or genuine error?

**Hook (0-4s):** "An unfamiliar name on your credit report is not automatically an error."

**Body (5-33s):** "The name might be a lender's trading name, a broker, a debt purchaser or a related company. First, compare the date, amount and account type with your own records. Then label the item: confirmed correct, needs more information, or supported error. Only ask for a correction when you can explain the exact field that appears wrong and what evidence supports the correction."

**Close (34-45s):** "My free checklist gives you that three-part review process without promising magic deletions."

**On-screen text:** Correct | Investigate | Supported error

**CTA:** Use the classification section of the free checklist.

## 3. What to record before requesting a correction

**Hook (0-4s):** "A vague complaint is hard to investigate. Build a clean evidence trail."

**Body (5-34s):** "Write down the reporting body, report date, exact account or entry, the field you believe is wrong, the correct information and the documents you hold. When you contact the reporting body or credit provider, record the date and reference number. Keep every response. Correction services are free, and if the issue is not resolved, Moneysmart explains the free complaint pathway."

**Close (35-47s):** "Use the evidence log in my free Australian Credit Report Review Checklist, and never post your report or identity documents in public."

**On-screen text:** Exact field | Correct value | Evidence | Reference number

**CTA:** Print or save the free checklist.

## Publishing notes

- Format: 9:16 vertical, 40-50 seconds.
- First frame: Katy's face plus the hook in large captions.
- Proof shot: official OAIC/Moneysmart page or the checklist, never a customer's data.
- Description disclosure: "General educational information only. No guaranteed credit outcome."
- Tracking: use a distinct UTM link per video and compare qualified checklist visits, not views alone.
